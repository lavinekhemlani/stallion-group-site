The group

Our values

Six principles that have governed how the group operates since 1969.

Local production

We build capacity where we operate. Milling, assembly and manufacturing happen in the markets we serve, not somewhere cheaper.

Long horizons

The group has never been built for the quarter. Investments are made for the decade that follows, and often for the generation after it.

Integrity in dealing

A trading house survives on its word. Contracts are honoured, obligations are met, and reputation is treated as the group's principal asset.

Technical excellence

Global standards, applied locally — ISO-certified mills, international assembly quality, and modern plant across every division.

Responsibility to community

The measure of an industrial group is the condition of the towns around its plants. Education, healthcare and livelihoods are part of the operating model, not an afterthought.

People first

The group's businesses are built by people who stay. We invest in talent across West Africa and the Middle East, and promote from within.

Work with Stallion

Distributors, suppliers, manufacturers, investors and candidates — we will direct your enquiry to the right desk within one business day.
