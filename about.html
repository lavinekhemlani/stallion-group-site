About the group

A family trading house that became an industrial group.

Stallion is a people's company, dedicated to its customers, partners and the communities around its facilities in West Africa. It has worked in the Sub-Saharan region for over fifty years, developing businesses across agriculture, food, commodities, automobiles, logistics, plastics, packaging and business services.

The group has invested significantly in building large-scale local industry in Africa — deploying modern technologies, generating employment and strongly endorsing the concept of domestic production. Today it operates around fifty companies across eighteen countries, from rice mills in northern Nigeria to an integrated steel plant near Warri, cold-storage facilities in seven cities and vehicle assembly lines in Lagos.

The group has been headquartered in the United Arab Emirates since 2003, with its African headquarters in Lagos and further operations in Ghana. It remains owned and led by the family that founded it.

Vision

To be Africa's leading company by adopting global best practices and localising them to develop scalable, impactful and sustainable businesses that are committed to improving the socio-economic conditions of the communities we serve.

Mission

To embrace local production, talent and people in achieving Africa's unlimited potential. To serve and satisfy customers with innovative offerings, high service orientation and excellent technical competency. To adopt high ethical and moral standards for sustainable business growth.
