Fish & Cold Chain

Aquaculture, processing, frozen fish and cold storage

7Cold-storage cities
2016–2019Best Fish Farmer of the Year
#1Tilapia producer in Nigeria

Stallion operates an integrated fish business comprising hatcheries, nurseries, grow-out ponds and cages, fish processing facilities and cold rooms. The group is the largest producer of Tilapia in Nigeria and was named Best Fish Farmer of the Year in 2016, 2017, 2018 and 2019.

Alongside domestic aquaculture, the division imports herring, horse mackerel, mackerel, croaker, sardinella and blue whiting from Europe, Asia and other parts of Africa, distributed through one of Africa's largest cold-storage networks.
