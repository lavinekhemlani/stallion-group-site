Steel & Mines

Integrated steel production and iron-ore mining

1.3mTonnes installed capacity
WarriDelta State
IntegratedDRI and steel making

Stallion Group operates the foremost integrated steel plant in the Sub-Saharan region and is the largest producer of high-quality steel in Nigeria, with an installed capacity of 1.3 million metric tonnes. The plant is situated near Warri in Delta State.

The division is supported by the group's own mining interests in iron ore, and is undergoing reactivation in partnership with the Federal Ministry of Steel Development.

BS4449 rebarReinforcement bar produced to British Standard 4449.
