Our history

From a family trading house in 1969 to an industrial group across eighteen countries.

1969
The foundingThe Vaswani family begins trading rice, wheat and sugar in West Africa.
1983
Stallion IndustriesThe business takes the Stallion name and expands beyond commodity trade.
1990s
AutomotiveExclusive distributorships bring global automotive marques to Nigeria.
2003
Dubai headquartersThe group establishes its global headquarters in the United Arab Emirates.
2010
Vehicle assemblyAssembly operations begin in Lagos, supporting domestic manufacturing.
2012
PackagingStallion Plastics Industries is established with 550 MT monthly capacity.
2015
SteelThe group takes on the foremost integrated steel plant in the region, near Warri.
2019
The InitiativeSarina Vaswani founds the Stallion Empowerment Initiative.
2021
Electric vehiclesThe first electric vehicle assembled in Nigeria is produced at the Lagos plant.
2026
A new chapterSteel reactivation is agreed with the Ministry of Steel Development, and Stallion.AI is established in Dubai.
