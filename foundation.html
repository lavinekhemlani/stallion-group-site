The Stallion Empowerment Initiative is the philanthropic endeavour of Stallion Group, founded by Sarina Vaswani in 2019. It was introduced in Nigeria with the intention of achieving lasting socio-economic impact across West Africa, and works through three pillars.

Education

Sub-Saharan Africa carries among the highest illiteracy rates in the world. SEI supports schooling and literacy programmes in the communities around the group's operations, on the principle that every business the group builds depends on the talent available around it.

Healthcare

SEI operates a clinic and a fifty-bed hospital with dedicated wards for women's and paediatric care in the Aladja region of Warri, Delta State, serving communities near the group's steel operations.

Youth empowerment

Through a public-private partnership model, SEI has trained approximately 40,000 rice farmers across 391 villages in twelve rice-producing states — improving yields, incomes and the reliability of local supply.
