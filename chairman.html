From the Chairman

Chairman's message

When my family began trading in West Africa in 1969, we dealt in rice, wheat and sugar. We had no plants, no assembly lines and no mills. What we had was a conviction that this region would one day make what it consumed — and that whoever helped build that capacity would build something durable.

Fifty-two years later, Stallion mills rice in the north of Nigeria, farms and processes fish, assembles vehicles in Lagos, manufactures packaging and produces steel near Warri. Very little of that was obvious when we started. It required patience of a kind that public markets rarely reward, and a willingness to invest in places others were not yet willing to.

We have never built for the quarter. We build for the generation that follows.

That patience has a purpose beyond the balance sheet. Every mill and every plant is also a payroll, a supply chain of farmers, and a town that grows around it. Our responsibility to those communities is not separate from the business — it is the reason the business is worth building.

The group now enters a new phase. We are investing in the reactivation of our steel operations, in modern technology across every division, and in a generation of leaders — within our family and outside it — who will carry this further than we have. The principles do not change: build locally, honour your word, and take the long view.

Sunil Vaswani
Chairman, Stallion Group
